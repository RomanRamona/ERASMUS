Democratic Sparks: Youth Igniting Change

Youth Participation Activities (KA154-YOU) - Erasmus+

Empowering young people to take an active part in democracy, strengthen media literacy and critical thinking, and tackle disinformation in their communities.

Project No. 2025-3-RO01-KA154-YOU-000376196
Period: 31.05.2026 - 30.03.2027

About the project

Democratic Sparks brings together young people from Romania, Poland and Türkiye to explore democratic participation, media literacy and civic engagement. Through mobility, dialogue and youth-led events, participants will develop the skills to challenge disinformation and contribute to stronger, more inclusive democracies.
